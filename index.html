Step by Step
How Rapid Lister Works
Three automated phases handle the entire listing process for you.
1

Fill The Form in the Extension

Open the Rapid Lister popup and fill in your product title, price, description, category, condition, availability, quantity, and tags. Upload your product photos from your PC.

2

Click "Manage Listings" to Auto-Fill

The extension navigates to Facebook Marketplace, waits for each field to load, and fills everything in automatically using React-compatible input simulation. Your photos are injected directly into the uploader.

3

Set Location & Post

Use the Location tab to set your city or ZIP code with smart autocomplete. Review the listing and submit — your item goes live on Facebook Marketplace in seconds.
